Privacy Policy

Your phone and Mac

Anywhere is provided by Fushinn. The app uses an encrypted connection to the Mac you choose, through your local network or a remote tunnel you configure. We do not operate a conversation relay or model service and do not receive your workspace content.

Where content goes

Text, selected images, files and commands you send go to your Mac. Codex and the model or tool services configured on your Mac may process that content and project context. Online services may send content to their respective providers. Check the provider, permissions and privacy terms on your Mac before connecting.

Storage on your device

Computer connections, drafts, attachments and preferences are stored on this device. Device keys and saved desktop credentials are stored in Keychain. Removing a computer clears its local connection, credentials and draft attachments. It does not delete copies on your Mac or other services.

Camera, photos and speech

The camera scans connection codes. Photos and files are read only after you select them. Speech is transcribed into a draft on the device; audio is not sent to your Mac. Text is sent only when you choose Send. The system may download speech resources from Apple. You can revoke access in system settings.

Desktop and Live Activities

Remote desktop transfers screen images and input between your phone and Mac. Live Activities can receive task phases, times and counts through Apple Push Notification service. Your Mac receives an activity identifier and push token. Push payloads do not contain conversation text or connection credentials.

External images

When you choose to load an external image, your phone contacts its website. That website may receive your IP address and the image URL. The request does not include your Mac credentials.

Retention and deletion

Unsent drafts and attachments remain on the device until you send them, remove the attachment or remove the computer. Conversation history is stored by the service on your Mac. You can withdraw connection consent in connection settings, and manage or delete remote data on your Mac and with the relevant provider.

Contact and updates

We do not add advertising tracking or behavioral analytics. Support emails you choose to send are used to handle your feedback and reply, and are kept only as needed to resolve the issue and meet legal obligations. Contact admin@jintongshu.com for privacy questions or deletion requests. Updated November 2, 2026. Material changes to data use require renewed consent.

This website

This website uses no analytics or advertising scripts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process access information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
