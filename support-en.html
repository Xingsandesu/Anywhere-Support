Connect your Mac

  1. Open AnywhereHost on your Mac.
  2. Choose Connect iPhone, then scan its code or paste its connection link in Anywhere.
  3. Keep your Mac awake and connected to the network.
Get AnywhereHost

Try it first

Choose Try it from the welcome screen. Conversation, dual-screen, tent and desktop examples work without a Mac.

Cannot connect?

Check that AnywhereHost is open and your network or configured tunnel is reachable. Generate a fresh connection code if the previous code has expired.

Contact

Include your app version, device, system version and the steps that reproduce the issue. Do not send pairing links, keys or private workspace content.
